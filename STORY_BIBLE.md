# Cathy’s Memory Arcade Story Bible

This file is the continuity and writing contract for every story added to the arcade. It applies to the Signal Theater serial, the branching After Closing files, game chapter copy, and future characters based on people Chad knows.

## The Story Engine

The explicit `BUT / THEREFORE` test comes from Trey Parker and Matt Stone’s NYU writing talk, not from the Duffer Brothers. The useful companion lesson from the Duffers is to build the genre spectacle around characters the audience wants to stay with.

- Never connect major beats with only “and then.” A beat must complicate what came before (`BUT`) or follow from it (`THEREFORE`).
- Every choice promises a consequence. The next scene must show that consequence in action, dialogue, inventory, relationship, route, or ending.
- Coincidence may start trouble, but it cannot solve trouble.
- A visual reveal is not a story turn until it changes what a character wants, knows, risks, or chooses.
- Comedy releases pressure without canceling emotion. Jokes come from character: Rook performs confidence, Cat names everything, and Runt makes the nickname look increasingly ridiculous.
- Endings answer the emotional question, not just the mechanical objective. Leave one honest door open, but finish the journey the player began.

Research references:

- [Matt Stone and Trey Parker’s “but / therefore” explanation at NYU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vGUNqq3jVLg)
- [The Duffer Brothers on beginning with characters, outlining the full season, and maintaining a mythology document](https://www.creativescreenwriting.com/cswcms/entering-the-upside-down-the-duffer-brothers-discuss-the-new-season-of-stranger-things-part-1/)

## Scene Card

Before prose or art is approved, every scene must answer these seven lines:

1. `POV`: Who owns this scene?
2. `WANT`: What do they need right now?
3. `BECAUSE`: What previous choice or event forced this moment?
4. `BUT`: What makes the obvious path impossible or emotionally costly?
5. `THEREFORE`: What decision creates the next scene?
6. `IMAGE`: What single composition tells most of the beat without text?
7. `HUMAN NOTE`: What gesture, joke, flaw, or mercy keeps the scene from reading like plot machinery?

Screen copy should usually stay under 30 words. Longer prose belongs in optional briefings, discoveries, or endings, never in a block that hides the art.

## Truth Boundary

The arcade combines family memory and original fantasy, so the line between them must remain visible.

- Biographical claims require a family source or Chad’s explicit account.
- A real likeness requires explicit authorization and a recorded source asset.
- Fantasy dialogue and actions are imagined; they must not be presented as quotations or historical events.
- Do not make a loved one flawless. Preserve wit, contradiction, agency, and ordinary human texture.
- Do not infer the appearance of a living person from a relative. Use an original fantasy design until a reference and permission are provided.

### Likeness Continuity

- `signal-story-03-cat-v1.webp` is Cat's approved identity anchor. Later scenes must preserve her long light-brown hair, long/oval face, open smile, and natural young-adult features rather than merely reusing her costume.
- `signal-story-04-runt-v1.webp` is Runt's approved story-world identity anchor, grounded in Chad's supplied portrait. Later scenes must preserve his round face, close smiling eyes, broad nose, full cheeks, short white beard, dark cap, and warm open smile.
- Identity is reviewed separately from lighting, costume, scale, and medium. A beautiful frame still fails if either face becomes generic.
- Private source photographs remain outside the shipped site. Generated derivatives must be labeled as fantasy likenesses, never historical photographs.

## Signal Theater Canon

### Coda

An adult clay wanderer who wakes in Cabinet 86 with two brass tokens and no past. She begins by treating memory as something to recover. She ends by learning that belonging can be built forward.

### Rook

A small brass raven with flexible ethics and excellent timing. He wants to look indispensable. He needs to learn that being invited is better than being needed.

### Tallow

A stone tollkeeper who asks for truth but responds best to nerve. His laugh literally opens doors.

### Cat

A fantasy character using Catherine’s family-authorized young-adult likeness. The real grounding details are Chad’s: she connected naturally with animals, plants, and children; kept a playful streak; and gave people nicknames. In the fantasy, Cat can wake anything that grows and names strangers into the family. Her words and magic are imagined, not biographical claims.

### Runt

A fantasy character using the user-supplied, authorized adult likeness of Catherine’s youngest brother. Catherine called him Runt even though he grew to be the largest sibling. In the fantasy he is physically enormous, careful with small living things, and funniest when nobody explains the joke. His giant body, mountain gate, and actions are imagined rather than biographical claims.

### Vesper

A midnight dragon who did not steal the final note. She trapped it because she was afraid of what silence comes after an ending.

## The Road Beyond Free Play

1. Coda wakes with two impossible tokens, **but** one calls from beyond the mountains; **therefore** she follows the moonroad.
2. Tallow demands a true memory, **but** Coda has none; **therefore** Rook risks a joke and Tallow’s laugh opens a new door.
3. The door reaches Cat, **but** even she cannot wake the silenced garden; **therefore** she sends them to Runt.
4. Runt can lift the gate, **but** a tiny nest is trapped below it; **therefore** Coda protects the nest and earns the path forward.
5. The garden holds forgotten songs, **but** midnight is consuming them; **therefore** Coda plants a token and grows a chorus.
6. The chorus reaches Vesper, **but** she guards the final note because she fears endings; **therefore** Coda offers her a place in the next verse instead of defeating her.
7. They reach a doorless arcade, **but** the tokens were never admission; **therefore** Coda uses them as hinges and opens the floor to everyone.
8. The floor is open, **but** four unanswered tones sound above it; **therefore** Cat and Runt prepare four unnamed lamps without guessing who belongs there.

## The Six-Sibling World

The long arc uncovers Catherine and her five siblings one at a time. This is an expandable world, not a single exposition dump.

- **Season One: Cat & Runt** discovers two sibling signals and leaves four protected, unnamed lights.
- Each later sibling receives an entrance, a contradiction, a consequential choice, a visual language, and a musical motif before joining the ensemble.
- A new sibling is not invented from family resemblance. Wait for Chad to provide the name, memories, image, and details that make the person specific.
- Earlier characters continue changing when a sibling enters. A discovery must complicate the existing family, not pause the plot for a profile.
- The final scene of each season resolves its immediate emotional promise **but** reveals a consequence that opens the next signal; **therefore** the serial can grow without cheap cliffhangers.
- Full-story playback uses a short comic-book `BUT / THEREFORE` page turn and musical key change between chapter films. Individual chapters must still work alone.

## Branching Story Contract

The existing After Closing files already encode consequence through flags, inventory, relationships, callbacks, and distinct endings. Future edits must preserve these causal spines:

- **The Last Token:** Mae wants to close the arcade, **but** Cabinet Zero edits pain out of memory; **therefore** every route asks what honesty costs and who gets to choose an ending.
- **Neon Runner 1986:** Rook wants to restore power quickly, **but** the old grid decides which blocks matter; **therefore** speed, trust, and distribution reshape the city that wakes.
- **The Cabinet That Remembers:** Mara wants a defensible answer, **but** the machine erases contradictory witnesses; **therefore** each investigative choice decides whether certainty or an honest record survives.

For a branch to remain in the game, it must produce a visible callback before the ending and make at least one later option, relationship, item, route, scene, or outro meaningfully different.
